## Proposed reception changes · not applied

- Maison Margiela: Keep positive provisionally; reduce confidence from high to medium because DSCENE supplies a materially more critical counter-reading.
- Courrèges: Consider positive → neutral / mixed at medium confidence: the rechecked Vogue review emphasises provisional judgement. A single review is insufficient to apply that change automatically.
- Vaquera: Consider neutral / mixed → positive at low confidence: Vogue is favourable about the performance and authenticity, but the critical sample remains limited.
- Isabel Marant: Consider positive → neutral / mixed at medium confidence: Vogue explicitly finds the collection tentative despite appreciating its lighter mood and craft.
- Chanel: Initial candidate: neutral / mixed, low confidence. Enthusiastic product-focused press and a divided small Reddit sample do not support a high-confidence overall verdict.
- Louis Vuitton: Initial candidate: positive, low confidence from the favourable Vogue review. Celebrity-outfit discussion is not sufficient collection-level audience evidence.

## Research limits

This is a collection-reporting recheck, not a claim that every historical biography or source in the original shelf was revalidated. Those source dates remain October 2. Vogue pages for Dior, Matières Fécales, Margiela and Balenciaga were inaccessible through the research reader; readable ELLE, Numéro or DSCENE articles were used. Weinsanto uses the indexed WWD/Yahoo syndicated report, counted as one source.

Chanel’s small r/chanel photo thread and the Dries collection thread were rechecked and labelled as self-selected samples. Louis Vuitton celebrity-outfit posts are excluded from runway reception. Other approved social samples remain explicitly dated October 2; they are not presented as newly surveyed audience opinion. No official Saint Laurent or Chloé departure is established by the checked reporting.
